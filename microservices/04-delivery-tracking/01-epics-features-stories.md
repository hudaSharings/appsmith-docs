## Tier legend
Every feature below is tagged with a delivery tier — how it relates to cross-service dependencies. Full rationale: [Parallel Delivery & Dependency Management](03-parallel-delivery-and-dependencies.md).
| Tier | Meaning | Assign when |
|---|---|---|
| **0** | No cross-service dependency | Immediately — unlimited parallelism |
| **1** | Needs one contract (an OpenAPI stub or event schema), not the whole upstream service | Immediately, against the mock — its first story is a **Contract Stub** (`Highest` priority) that freezes the contract in 1-3 points |
| **2** | Needs a real (not mocked) upstream — an intra-service dependency or verified real behaviour | Only once the specific blocking work is genuinely done |
